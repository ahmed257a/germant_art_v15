# germant_art_v8

## Version 15

### Question and study updates

- Choose whether recall questions show the German word or its English meaning.
- Choose between word recall and sentence recall, where a missing German word is typed into an example sentence.
- Show or hide example sentences during the study preview.
- Questions no longer advance automatically. Submit an answer with the Submit button or Enter, review the result, then select **Next** to continue.
- After submitting or timing out, review the correct German word, English translation, and German and English example sentences.
- The German word remains visible in the study interface, and speaker buttons pronounce German words and example sentences. Manual pronunciation works even when automatic audio is muted.
- Question preferences are stored separately in browser local storage. Existing progress files and their format are unchanged.
